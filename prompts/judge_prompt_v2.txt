Task: You are annotating sentiment in Bhojpuri text (Devanagari script; the
sentence may include code-switched Hindi or other words). For the sentence
given below, produce a two-stage sentiment label and return it as a single
strict JSON object — no prose, no markdown fences, nothing besides the JSON.

Step 1 (subjectivity). Decide whether the sentence is:
- "objective" — a factual report, event description, or plain narration,
  carrying no evaluative stance from the author, even if it uses emotionally
  loaded words (routine news writing often does this).
- "subjective" — the sentence carries an evaluative or emotional stance:
  an opinion, feeling, praise, complaint, judgment, or reaction, whether from
  the author or a quoted speaker.

Step 2 (polarity) — fill this in only when Step 1 = "subjective":
- "positive" — favorable, appreciative, hopeful, celebratory stance.
- "negative" — critical, sorrowful, angry, fearful, disapproving stance.
- "neutral" — a stance is present but it is mild, hedged, or has no clear
  positive/negative charge.
- "mixed" — a positive component and a negative component are both clearly
  present, with neither dominant.

Constraint: when Step 1 = "objective", the polarity field must be JSON null;
polarity must never be null when Step 1 = "subjective".

Watch for: negation (a positive word under negation expresses the opposite
sense), sarcasm/irony (surface praise can mean the opposite), metaphor and
devotional address (decode to the sentiment actually conveyed rather than
the literal image), and proverbs (a proverb with no specific addressed
target is "objective"; the same proverb aimed at a named person/group in
context is "subjective").

Respond with exactly this JSON shape and these keys, nothing more:

{
  "subjectivity": "objective" | "subjective",
  "polarity": "positive" | "negative" | "neutral" | "mixed" | null,
  "confidence": <float between 0 and 1>,
  "domain": "<one-to-three word normalized topic, e.g. politics, festival, family, farming>",
  "narrative_voice": "first_person" | "third_person" | "mixed",
  "sentiment_target": "<short text naming who/what the sentiment is directed at, or null>",
  "rationale": "<exactly one sentence explaining the label>"
}
